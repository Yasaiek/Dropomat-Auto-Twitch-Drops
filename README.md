## What it does

- **Pick and queue** – Open drop campaigns in one list. Click the games in the order you want – that is your queue.
- **Watches by itself** – Opens a muted, minimized window, picks a stream where the drops for that game really run, and moves on when it ends.
- **Tracks new drops** – Star a game: when a new campaign appears, it joins the queue and starts on its own.
- **Shows real progress** – Reads progress from your Twitch account, shows every drop with its icon and changes stream when progress stalls.
- **Optional auto-claim** – Can claim earned drops for you – only when Twitch confirms the game account is linked.
- **12 languages** – The whole interface in 12 languages, switchable at any time.

## Install in a minute

1. Download the ZIP and unpack it into a folder you are going to keep.
2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode (top right).
3. Click “Load unpacked” and choose the unpacked folder.
4. Log in on twitch.tv, open Dropomat, click the games you want and press Start.

<p align="center"><img src="docs/img/install.png" alt="" width="820"></p>

> Works in Chrome, Edge, Opera, Brave and other Chromium browsers.

### Updating

Replace the files in the folder and press ⟳ next to Dropomat on chrome://extensions. Your queue and settings stay.

## Good to know

**Do I have to keep the stream on screen?**  
No. It plays in its own muted window that can stay minimized while you play.

**Does it claim drops for me?**  
Only if you turn that on in Settings. Otherwise the game is marked “To claim” and the queue moves on.

**Is it safe for my account?**  
Automated watching is against Twitch's terms of service, so you use it at your own risk. Your login never leaves your browser.

**Why is a campaign missing from the list?**  
Press ⟳ in the Campaigns tab. If it is still missing, add the game by name in the Watching tab or track it.
